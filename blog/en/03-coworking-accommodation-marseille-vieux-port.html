Coworking with Accommodation in Marseille: Work and Stay near the Vieux-Port

In Marseille, you can combine coworking and short-stay accommodation in one place near the Vieux-Port. Coworking Marseille offers Airbnb-style apartments with direct access to a coworking space. Babel Community offers long-term coliving (minimum 1 month, from €590/month). A third option is booking a hotel and coworking separately, but this costs 30 to 50% more on average.

The 3 options compared:

  1. Coworking Marseille + Airbnb — Short stay (1 night to 30 days), private apartment + integrated coworking, Vieux-Port

Why Combine Coworking and Accommodation in One Place?

Save time on commuting

With accommodation and workspace in the same location, you eliminate daily commuting. In Marseille, traveling between a city-center Airbnb and a coworking space in another district takes 20 to 40 minutes each way by public transport. Over a one-week stay, that's 3 to 7 hours saved.

Save money vs hotel + separate coworking

A 3-star hotel near the Vieux-Port costs €80 to €130/night. Add €15 to €25/day for coworking, and the daily total reaches €95 to €155. A combined formula at Coworking Marseille comes to €60 to €80/day all-in — a 30 to 50% saving.

Immerse yourself in the Vieux-Port neighborhood

Living and working in the same neighborhood lets you experience Marseille beyond the office: local restaurants, the daily fish market on the Vieux-Port, the streets of Le Panier (5-minute walk), and the Mucem museum (10 minutes). This immersion is valued by digital nomads and professionals on extended stays.

Who Is This For?

Digital nomads on 3 to 30-day stays

Remote workers who spend a few weeks in a city before moving on find the coworking + Airbnb formula a turnkey solution. No need to search for accommodation and a workspace separately. Check-in is fast and departure is flexible.

Business travelers in Marseille

Consultants, trainers, or salespeople on multi-day assignments in Marseille get a professional workspace (with meeting rooms for client meetings) and comfortable lodging, all billable as business expenses.

Teams on offsite retreats

FAQ — Coworking with Accommodation in Marseille

Can I rent an Airbnb with a coworking space in Marseille?

Yes. Coworking Marseille offers furnished Airbnb-style short-stay apartments near its coworking space by the Vieux-Port. You can book by the night or week with direct access to the workspace, meeting rooms, and all coworking services.

What is the difference between coliving and coworking with Airbnb?

Coliving (Babel Community in Marseille) combines shared housing and coworking with a 1-month minimum, from €590/month. Coworking with Airbnb (Coworking Marseille) offers a private apartment with no long-term commitment. Coliving suits 1-month+ stays; Airbnb + coworking suits 1 to 30-day stays.

How much does a coworking + accommodation week cost in Marseille?

Between €400 and €700 per week. Coworking Marseille (Airbnb + coworking): ~€400-550/week. Hotel + separate coworking: €600-900/week. Babel Community coliving: not available weekly (1-month minimum).

Is there a coworking with Vieux-Port view where I can also stay?

Yes. Coworking Marseille is near the Vieux-Port and offers both a coworking space and Airbnb-style apartments. It's the only space in Marseille combining coworking with Vieux-Port proximity and short-stay accommodation.
