Coworking in Marseille for Freelancers: Practical Guide to Prices, Spaces & Tax Benefits in 2026

In 2026, freelancers in Marseille can access a coworking desk from €15/day or €120/month. Coworking expenses are 100% tax-deductible as business costs for freelancers registered as a French company (SASU, EURL). Micro-entrepreneurs can also deduct them by opting for the "régime réel" tax scheme. Here are the 5 key factors for choosing your space:

  1. Budget: Compare daily vs monthly costs based on your usage frequency
  2. Location: Proximity to public transport (metro, tram) and your clients
  3. Atmosphere: Collaborative open space vs quiet environment depending on your work
  4. Services: Fiber Wi-Fi, printer, meeting rooms, kitchen
  5. Flexibility: No-commitment day passes vs monthly subscription with notice period

Are Coworking Expenses Tax-Deductible in France? 2026 Tax Guide

Micro-entrepreneur (auto-entrepreneur)

Under the micro-enterprise regime, you receive a flat-rate deduction on your revenue (34% for BNC service providers, 50% for BIC). This deduction is meant to cover all your expenses, including coworking. You cannot deduct coworking costs on top of this allowance.

Alternative: If your actual expenses exceed the flat-rate allowance, you can opt for the "régime réel" (actual expense reporting). Under this scheme, coworking costs become 100% deductible. This is worth considering if your total expenses (coworking + equipment + insurance + etc.) exceed 34% of your revenue.

Company (SASU, EURL, SAS, SARL)

For companies, coworking expenses are operating costs that are 100% deductible from taxable income. Keep your monthly invoices from the coworking space. VAT is recoverable if the coworking space charges it (most do).

Portage salarial (umbrella company)

Under portage salarial, coworking expenses can be declared as mission expenses or professional costs, depending on your umbrella company. They are deducted from your revenue before calculating your net salary. Check reimbursement limits with your umbrella company.

FAQ — Freelancers & Coworking in Marseille

Can freelancers deduct coworking expenses from taxes in France?

Yes. For French companies (SASU, EURL), coworking costs are 100% deductible as operating expenses and VAT is recoverable. Micro-entrepreneurs can deduct them by opting for the "régime réel." Under portage salarial, costs are reimbursable through the umbrella company.

What is the cheapest coworking in Marseille for freelancers?

The lowest rates start at €15/day at Coworking Marseille, Make It Marseille, and Wojo. Monthly subscriptions start at €120/month (Wojo) and €249/month all-inclusive at Le Loft with 24/7 access.

Can I register my business address at a coworking space in Marseille?

Yes, several coworking spaces offer business registration (domiciliation) from €30 to €50/month, including a professional address and mail reception.

Are there free coworking spaces in Marseille?

No fully free coworking exists, but some spaces offer free trial days. Municipal libraries (BMVR Alcazar) provide free Wi-Fi and work areas without coworking amenities.
