Meeting Room Rental in Marseille: Prices, Capacity & Available Spaces in 2026

In Marseille, meeting rooms rent for €25 to €90 per hour depending on capacity and equipment. The main venues offering hourly rooms are Coworking Marseille (Vieux-Port), Now Coworking (9 rooms, Vieux-Port), Hiptown (République), Babel Community (Joliette), and Wojo (Les Docks). Here's a comparison with prices, capacities, and equipment.

5 criteria to check before booking:

  1. Capacity: 4-6 people, 8-12 people, or 15+ people
  2. Equipment: Screen/TV, video conferencing, whiteboard, Wi-Fi
  3. Pricing: Hourly, half-day, or full-day rates
  4. Location: Metro/tram accessibility and parking
  5. Extra services: Coffee, catering, accommodation for out-of-town attendees

Other Meeting Rooms in Central Marseille

Now Coworking — 9 rooms at the Vieux-Port

The widest selection of rooms at the Vieux-Port: 9 meeting rooms for 4 to 30 people, some with panoramic harbor views. Full video conferencing equipment. Rates from €35 to €90/hr. Concierge and catering service available on request.

Hiptown — République

Modern rooms for 4 to 15 people in a startup environment. Ideal for client meetings and workshops. Phone booths available for individual calls. Rates from €30 to €60/hr.

Babel Community — Joliette

Meeting rooms in Marseille's business district. Community atmosphere with rooftop terrace for breaks. Competitive rates from €25 to €50/hr. On-site coliving available for long-stay participants.

Wojo — Les Docks

Nationwide network with rooms at Les Docks, Joliette. The advantage: bookings are possible across all Wojo locations in France via a single platform. Rates from €30 to €70/hr.

Meeting Rooms by Use Case

Team meeting (4-8 people)

A 4 to 8-seat room works for standard work meetings. Budget: €25-40/hr. Look for spaces with whiteboard and screen sharing. Average duration: 1 to 2 hours.

Training or workshop (10-20 people)

Training sessions need larger spaces with flexible layouts (U-shape, clusters, or classroom). Budget: €40-70/hr. Confirm the desired layout is available. Average duration: half-day to full day.

Event or conference (20+ people)

For large events, Now Coworking (up to 30 people) and Coworking Marseille (event space) offer the biggest capacities in the city center. Budget: €60-90/hr or day-rate packages. Catering available on request.

Multi-day meeting with accommodation for participants

If your meeting spans multiple days or your participants travel from other cities, Coworking Marseille is the only venue combining meeting rooms and short-stay accommodation. See our meeting + accommodation offer.

How to Book a Meeting Room in Marseille

  1. Define your needs: number of attendees, duration, required equipment
  2. Compare venues: use the comparison table above
  3. Contact the venue: by phone, email, or online form
  4. Confirm booking: most venues require a deposit or credit card
  5. Prepare your meeting: send presentations in advance if needed, arrive 10 min early

Equipment Checklist

  • High-speed Wi-Fi (fiber optic)
  • Screen or projector
  • Video conferencing system (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet)
  • Whiteboard or flipchart
  • Sufficient power outlets
  • Air conditioning / heating
  • Coffee, water, snacks
  • Nearby parking
  • Wheelchair accessibility

FAQ — Meeting Rooms in Marseille

How much does a meeting room cost per hour in Marseille?

Between €25 and €90 per hour. A 4-6 person room averages €25-40/hr. An 8-12 person room costs €40-60/hr. Spaces for 15+ people: €60-90/hr.

Where can I find a meeting room with a Vieux-Port view?

Now Coworking and Coworking Marseille both have rooms with Vieux-Port views. Now Coworking has 9 rooms, some with direct harbor views.

Can I book a meeting room on weekends in Marseille?

Some venues accept weekend bookings. Coworking Marseille and Le Loft (24/7 member access) offer weekend options. Contact the venue directly to confirm.

Can I rent a meeting room without a coworking membership?

Yes, most coworking spaces rent meeting rooms without requiring a membership. Booking is typically hourly or half-day with no commitment.

Are there meeting rooms with accommodation for attendees in Marseille?

Yes. Coworking Marseille is the only venue offering meeting rooms alongside Airbnb-style short-stay apartments. Ideal for multi-day seminars and offsites with out-of-town participants.
