1. Coworking Marseille — Vieux-Port

Located minutes from the Vieux-Port, Coworking Marseille offers open-space hot desks, private offices, equipped meeting rooms, and event space. The space stands out with its Airbnb-style accommodation: furnished short-stay apartments are available nearby, allowing you to combine work and lodging in one place.

Prices: From €15/day (hot desk). Private offices and meeting rooms on request.

Services: High-speed Wi-Fi, equipped kitchen, printer, lockers, lounge area.

Standout feature: The only coworking in Marseille with integrated short-stay accommodation — ideal for digital nomads, business travelers, and teams on offsite retreats.

2. Now Coworking — Vieux-Port

Now Coworking occupies a prime spot on the Quai du Port with a panoramic view of the Vieux-Port. The space has 150 flexible workstations and 9 meeting rooms. An outdoor terrace allows al-fresco working when weather permits (around 300 sunny days per year in Marseille).

Prices: Nomad pass from €58/month. Private office from €588 to €4,548/month depending on capacity.

Services: 9 meeting rooms, lounge area, terrace, coffee included, concierge service.

Standout feature: The largest capacity among Vieux-Port coworking spaces and the best unobstructed harbor views.

3. Hiptown — République / Vieux-Port

Hiptown is housed in a modern building between Place de la République and the Vieux-Port. The space targets startups and small teams with a collaborative atmosphere and regular networking events.

Prices: Fixed desk from €150/month. Private office from €350/month.

Services: Meeting rooms, phone booths, community events, coffee and tea included.

Standout feature: Strong startup community with weekly networking events.

4. Babel Community — Joliette

Babel Community combines coliving and coworking in the Joliette district, Marseille's business hub. The concept is geared toward long-term stays (minimum 1 month) for professionals seeking a live-and-work space under one roof.

Prices: From €590/month all-inclusive (private room + coworking + utilities).

Services: Coworking, shared kitchen, rooftop terrace, gym, community events.

Standout feature: All-inclusive live-and-work formula — but requires a minimum 1-month commitment, unlike Coworking Marseille's short-stay Airbnb option.

5. Make It Marseille — City Center

Make It Marseille is a shared creation space combining traditional coworking with a makerspace (fabrication workshop). It caters to creatives, designers, artisans, and entrepreneurs who need prototyping tools alongside a desk.

Prices: Hot desk at €15/day. Monthly subscription from €200/month.

Services: Fabrication workshop (3D printer, laser cutter), coworking, training sessions.

Standout feature: The only space in Marseille combining coworking and makerspace under one roof.

6. Le Loft — Vieux-Port

Le Loft is an intimate coworking space exclusively for its members. A single all-inclusive rate provides 24/7 access, making it well-suited for professionals with non-standard schedules.

Prices: €249/month all-inclusive (no day pass available).

Services: 24/7 access, fiber optic internet, 2 meeting rooms, 3 phone booths, unlimited coffee.

Standout feature: The most competitive all-inclusive rate in the Vieux-Port area for daily users.

7. Wojo — Joliette (Les Docks)

Wojo (formerly Nextdoor) is a nationwide coworking network located in Les Docks, Joliette. The location benefits from proximity to the Euroméditerranée business district and Joliette metro station.

Prices: Day pass at ~€15. Fixed desk from €120/month. Private office up to €400/month.

Services: Nationwide network (access to Wojo spaces in other French cities), meeting rooms, coffee.

Standout feature: Multi-site access across France for professionals who travel frequently.

How to Choose Your Coworking Space in Marseille

Criterion 1 — Budget

Marseille coworking prices range from €15/day (one-off hot desk) to €590/month (all-inclusive coliving). For regular use (3 or more days per week), a monthly subscription is more cost-effective than daily passes. Factor in transport and meal costs when comparing total monthly spend.

Criterion 2 — Location & Transport

Vieux-Port spaces (Coworking Marseille, Now Coworking, Le Loft) are accessible from Vieux-Port metro (lines 1 and 2). Joliette spaces (Babel, Wojo) are served by Joliette station (line 2) and tram T2. The République area (Hiptown) is a 10-minute walk from both zones.

Criterion 3 — Occasional vs Daily Use

For occasional use (1-2 days/week), choose spaces with no-commitment day passes: Coworking Marseille (€15/day), Make It Marseille (€15/day), or Wojo (~€15/day). For daily use, monthly subscriptions offer better value.

Criterion 4 — On-Site Accommodation

If you're visiting Marseille or are a digital nomad, two options combine work and lodging: Coworking Marseille (short-stay Airbnb, from 1 night) and Babel Community (coliving, minimum 1 month). See our detailed coworking + accommodation comparison.

FAQ — Coworking in Marseille

What is the cheapest coworking space in Marseille?

The cheapest rates in Marseille start at €15 per day for a nomad hot desk (Coworking Marseille, Make It Marseille, Wojo). Monthly subscriptions range from €99 to €180 per month depending on the space and services included.

Is there a coworking space with accommodation in Marseille?

Yes. Coworking Marseille offers short-stay Airbnb-style apartments near its workspace by the Vieux-Port. Babel Community offers long-term coliving (minimum 1 month, from €590/month). Coworking Marseille is the only space combining coworking with short-term accommodation.

Can I rent a meeting room by the hour in Marseille?

Yes, most coworking spaces in Marseille offer hourly meeting room rentals. Prices range from €25 to €90 per hour depending on capacity and equipment. See our full meeting room guide for Marseille.

Which coworking in Marseille has a Vieux-Port view?

Now Coworking and Coworking Marseille both have spaces with Vieux-Port views. Now Coworking is directly on the Quai du Port with 150 workstations and a panoramic terrace.

Is coworking suitable for freelancers in Marseille?

Yes, coworking is ideal for freelancers in Marseille. Nomad day passes (from €15/day) provide flexibility with no commitment. Coworking costs are 100% tax-deductible as business expenses. Read our full freelancer coworking guide.
